This week's new features

RPE and wellness now have their own page

Hello! Your players already answer: the morning check-in, and RPE after a session. Those answers were already feeding your load, your signals and your brief — but to actually read them, you had to go looking. Each now has its own page under Tracking. Plus a nudge on the player side, and a reminder of where to switch the check-in on 👇

Tracking · new page

📊 RPE had the answers. Now it has a page.

Your players were already rating their sessions, and those ratings were already computing your load. What was missing was somewhere to look at them. That's the RPE page: four figures up top — players' average RPE, yours, the gap between the two, response rate — and a grid below, one row per player, one column per session.

The Gap column is the one to read first: it tells you who found the session harder — or easier — than you planned it. Filter over 7 days, 4 weeks or 12 weeks, and isolate training sessions or matches.
❤️

Tracking · wellness

Wellness goes from one day to seven

Same story on the wellness side: the answers were there, but the page only showed today's. Now every player in the active squad has a row across 7 days, and whoever is slipping rises to the top — "fatigue rising for 3 days", "stress rising for 3 days". A player drifting slowly doesn't show up on a single day.
🔔

Player app

Today's check-in stops slipping through

As long as a player hasn't done today's check-in, they see a card at the top of their agenda and a dot on their Fitness tab. One tap and it's done — the reminder disappears. Enough to pick up the answers from those who forget, without you chasing anyone.
 
🗓️

Reminder · the check-in

Not using the check-in yet? Here's what it is

It's a short fitness questionnaire — fatigue, sleep, pain, mood, stress. A few seconds for the player, and a daily read on the state of the squad for you. It goes out to the whole active squad, with no call-up or attendance to confirm, by WhatsApp, email or the app.

These are the answers that feed the Wellness page above. RPE is asked after a session — a different question, at a different moment.

And you switch it on straight from your calendar, on the week grid: every day has its own little chip. By default everything is Off — nothing goes out until you turn a day on. Tick only the days you actually want to ask the squad: one check-in per player per day.
