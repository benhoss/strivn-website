GPS module

📥 Your GPS imports, under control

Import up to 10 files at once for the same session — no more dragging them in one by one. A file matched to the wrong player? Reassign it in one click from the matching screen. And if a data point looks off after import — even weeks later — fix it directly, no need to re-import everything.

 
🗺️

Live match

Pinpoint the action on the pitch, live

On a shot, a turnover, an interception… tap the spot on the pitch where it happened while logging the event live. After the session, find a zone map in the analysis — to see where your team really creates and concedes.
🔥

Post-match analysis

A page dedicated to the full match analysis

The zone map now crosses match stats with each player's GPS data, with a heatmap-style density view. See at a glance where — and how — the match was really played.
🔄

Live training

Switch drills without breaking the session

Sessions don't always go to plan. From the live screen, change the next block in one tap — run the plan in a different order, or pull from your backup drills — without breaking the rhythm.
 
🔓

Exercise library

Your exercises, finally shareable

Every exercise in your library now has its own visibility — public for the whole team, private while you're building it.

And to step outside Strivn: a public share link, viewable without an account or login — perfect for dropping into a WhatsApp group.
