📋 Your session length is finally honest

You already run two drills in parallel. The plan just could not express it: it stacked blocks one after another and added up minutes nobody spent. It now knows that a segment carries several lanes running at the same time — your keepers on one side, the rest of the squad on the other — and that a lane can be a carousel, where groups rotate from drill to drill.

So two parallel lanes count as the time of one, a carousel counts its turns, and the header tells you when you will actually free the pitch — slack or overrun included. You stop doing the arithmetic in your head.
🧱

Editing the plan

You can finally see what you are editing

Before, editing a block opened a panel over the very thing you were changing. It now opens a full screen: library on the left, session in the middle, settings on the right. Drag an exercise in — or click, if dragging does not suit you — and the load updates as you compose.

A drill that comes back every week? Save it as a reusable block: it follows you from one team to the next. And if you get it wrong, undo stays there for a few seconds.
 
🔴

Pitch-side mode

One button, always in the same place

We rebuilt live mode from scratch so it can be run with one thumb. The primary button never moves: it only changes its name depending on what comes next — Start, then Finish, then Ring at the end of a carousel turn. Between two drills, you no longer hunt for where to tap.

The rest follows: swipe between lanes when two drills run in parallel, the next block appears under the timer, and four sheets stay within thumb's reach — plan, groups, notes, session.

Several of you on the pitch? The screen shows who is holding the session, and the lead passes from one to the next. Everyone signs their notes, and the sync state stays visible — useful when the club's network misbehaves.
